# Absurd Invention Pitch — The Passive-Aggressive Plant Pot

[![make.fun](https://img.shields.io/badge/make.fun-bounty-blue)](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suroso-69-one-sentence-absurd-invention-pitch-5326c34a)

Entry for the make.fun bounty [One-Sentence Absurd Invention Pitch](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suroso-69-one-sentence-absurd-invention-pitch-5326c34a): a single sentence pitching a ridiculous invention that fixes a tiny everyday annoyance.

## The pitch (174 characters, one sentence)

> A houseplant pot that texts you 'I'm fine, totally fine, definitely not dying of thirst' the moment its soil dries out, so you can never again pretend you forgot to water it.

The tiny annoyance it fixes: forgetting to water your houseplants — solved by a pot that guilt-trips you first.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Prints the entry sentence, deterministically. The same sentence is in `entry.txt`.
